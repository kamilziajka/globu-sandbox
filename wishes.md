5. Here is to a year of new adventures, good health and dreams coming true. Happy birthday!
6. Cheers to you on your special day. May every candle you blow out carry a wish that comes true.
7. Happy birthday to someone who makes the world a kinder place just by being in it.
8. May the year ahead be full of moments worth remembering and people worth sharing them with.
9. Wishing you a birthday that is every bit as special as you are, today and all year long.
10. Happy birthday! Thank you for being you. Enjoy every minute of your day.
